# Demo video

`saved-clip.mp4` is a silent, 10-second excerpt of **Earth rotation during
Galileo flyby (PIA00114)**, created from real spacecraft photographs taken on
11–12 December 1990. Credit: **NASA/JPL**.

- Source and public-domain declaration: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Earth_rotation_during_Galileo_flyby_(PIA00114).webm>
- Original NASA catalog: <https://photojournal.jpl.nasa.gov/catalog/PIA00114>
- Download: <https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/87/Earth_rotation_during_Galileo_flyby_%28PIA00114%29.webm>
- License: **public domain (PD NASA)**, not CC-BY; Commons marks attribution
  as not required. No endorsement by NASA/JPL is implied.

The first 10 seconds were encoded as H.264/yuv420p at 24 fps, without audio
(the source and previous demo clip have no audio), with MP4 faststart. The
480×360 source was scaled proportionally to 960×720 and pillarboxed to
1280×720 rather than stretched or cropped. The result is 797,289 bytes.
